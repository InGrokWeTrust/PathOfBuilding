## Our practical allocation strategy

### Level 26–40: retain RF sustain and permanent life routes

- Continue from Scion along the life-oriented paths that appear in the target level-75 tree. Preserve Constitution and any similarly shared life/pathing passives **only if they are actually allocated in the current character**.
- Prioritize maximum life, necessary regeneration, and efficient connectors toward Templar/Marauder/Duelist areas over spending additional points in optional fire-only detours.
- Keep the minimum fire-only investment that makes RF + Fire Trap comfortable. Do **not** refund a critical sustain node prematurely.

### Level 40–55: build the shared defensive/Templar backbone

- Extend toward the **Sovereignty** side of the passive tree via life/armour/regen paths that also appear in the target setup.
- Take useful life and regeneration nodes on this path when they serve the current RF build.
- If additional damage is needed to finish acts efficiently, temporary RF nodes are acceptable; "zero refunds" is not worth making the campaign miserable.

### Level 55–65: start banking permanent aura efficiency

- Take **Sovereignty** and its useful connected reservation passives once a valid route exists. This investment remains valuable after RF.
- If your tree naturally approaches **Champion of the Cause** through its permanent lower/south-west routes, begin extending there; otherwise wait until enough points are available.
- Avoid committing points to optional off-route fire, attack or trap clusters without a measured PoB benefit.

### Level 65–75: prepare transition but do not abandon RF early

- Finish the permanent path to **Champion of the Cause**.
- Obtain access to **Empowered Bond** and the Link Mastery, but allocate their link-only payoff near the actual switch when **Flame Link** is used; they do not make RF clear the campaign faster.
- Prioritize permanent life and reservation nodes; defer **Inexorable** if short on points.
- Reserve actual refund points or Orbs of Regret for temporary fire/damage/sustain nodes that will no longer be needed.

## Refund classification rules

| Kind | Decision |
|---|---|
| Overlap: allocated both now and in Allie's level-75 target | **Keep** |
| Needed in Allie's target but not currently allocated | **Allocate along a connected path** |
| RF-only nodes absent from target | **Temporary; refund at conversion** |
| Temporary mastery choices | **Reevaluate at conversion** |
| RF sustain nodes that also support life/regen on the final tree | **Prefer to keep** |
| Weak, off-path detours | **Avoid unless a measured leveling benefit outweighs refund cost** |

Never refund a connector while it is required to keep other allocated nodes connected to the class start.

## Transition gate (level ~75, not a hard level requirement)

1. Campaign/required passive reward quests complete; passive points available.
2. Kineticist or Manyshot Mercenary acquired and reasonably equipped.
3. The exact proposed aurabot aura setup fits in PoB with realistic gear, reservation and available mana.
4. A viable link/mercenary combat plan, including defenses, is ready.
5. Confirm the RF-only nodes to refund without disconnecting retained nodes, then apply the respec in the correct order.
6. Plan **Golden Glory** as the final ascendancy allocation when ready for Uber Lab.
